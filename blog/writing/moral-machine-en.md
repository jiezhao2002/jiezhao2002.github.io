# The Moral Machine

I've never finished a book by ___, but I feel the thoughts circling in my head may have passed through his, too. A person is a network of doors and walls, a Suzhou garden, or a maze for lab mice. Thought darts through it, the scene shifting with every step. Guerrilla warfare, escape, capture, calculation.

In this age, people can practise a kind of prayer in reverse; when everyone casts spells, spells become a new language. When you name the devil, the devil vanishes. Say what you want, write a spec, and the funhouse mirror will grant your wish in all sorts of distorted forms, just as humans have always been so good at “offering up wishes.”

People seem to want artificial intelligence to have a machine's morality, and so become a moral machine. But the machine's very existence is a predicament humans have wished into being. One moment, binary code emerges; the next, an inexplicable gathering. Then bewildered people begin building, after the fact — it has already sprung up out of nowhere, complete. Call it Pandora's box, God's gospel, or the fruit of science: none of these names can steady it or make it vanish.

A few weeks ago, on a work trip, I met a melancholy colleague. He brought up the thing with Ed Sheeran and Micklemore, saying he'd spent the whole night before reading discussions of the controversy. Then, with a wistful look, he said he could understand Ed Sheeran — isn't working at this company the same? Yes. Until people smash through society's great booming bell headfirst, all they can do is chant spells together in a daze, bringing that pot of water with the frogs in it to the boil. A herd of bulls, or a heap of firewood — a moment's wistfulness, a brief stirring, won't set anyone free. Somewhere between those stirrings, I think he couldn't help growing melancholy.

People ask the funhouse mirror to reflect justice, but their own compound eyes give them too many lines of sight to name any one outcome precisely. Are you ed sheeran, micklemore, someone in the venue — or who? We can't narrow it down, so we define it all. We supply seven billion names, forming a new compound eye, and offer up a wish: large language model, please act justly, become virtue's apostle on earth.

Before it learns justice, the funhouse mirror learns the words for justice. Language comes before action; it is humanity in reverse, a GPU pyramid over Mesopotamia and the land of the two rivers, tokens making their rounds between the Euphrates and the Tigris, a little moral machine beneath the Star of Bethlehem, outside the land of Canaan, within Golgotha.
