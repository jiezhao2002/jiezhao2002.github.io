# Remains of a Shrimp

Back in 2021, my favorite music player Xiami stopped its service, I still think they had the best recommendation algorithms. Xiami == 虾米 == Shrimp, something lost in the translation but the analogy always remain, I thus thougt to make a banner for its remains that are still with me today.

I migrated my Xiami playlist later to Netease, to Apple Music, to Spotify, and to SoundCloud. A stack on intangible digital traces that always tail along with me.
